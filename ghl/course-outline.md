# Q4 Revenue Playbook — course outline for GoHighLevel

5 categories, 14 lessons. Written from the masterclass content, so this
is the paste source when you build the product in **Memberships → Products**.

GHL structure: **Product** = the course, **Category** = a module, **Lesson** = a lesson.

- **Product title:** The Q4 Revenue Playbook
- **Subtitle:** Build the quarter that decides your year.
- **Description:** The promotional calendar, the offers, the flows, and the paid social
  plan — built once, in order, before the holiday rush.

Mark the first lesson as free/preview if you want a public teaser.

---

## Category 1: Your Promotional Calendar

*Decide what runs when, so November is execution rather than invention.*

1. **Mapping the quarter on one page**  **(free preview)**
   Every promotion, every send, every creative deadline on a single page. If it does not fit on one page you will not follow it.
2. **Choosing your anchor promotions**
   Two or three anchors carry the quarter. Everything else supports them or gets cut.
3. **Setting your build-by date**
   The date everything must be finished. Working backwards from it is what protects December.

## Category 2: Offer Strategy and AOV

*Ways to sell that are not simply discounting harder than last year.*

1. **Selling beyond 25% off**
   Bundles, tiered discounts, gifts with purchase, and thresholds — what each one does to margin and to perceived value.
2. **Engineering a bigger basket**
   Threshold maths: setting the free-shipping and gift tiers against your real AOV distribution rather than a round number.
3. **Protecting margin while you scale**
   The discount depth that still leaves you a business in January.

## Category 3: Email and SMS

*The campaigns and the automated flows that carry the quarter.*

1. **The Q4 campaign calendar**
   Send cadence that increases without burning the list.
2. **Flows that must exist before October**
   Welcome, browse abandonment, cart and checkout recovery, post-purchase. In priority order, with the one most stores are missing.
3. **Segmenting for the season**
   Who hears about which offer, and who you deliberately leave out.

## Category 4: Paid Social

*Where Meta fits, and why CAC is not decided inside the ad account.*

1. **What your Q4 creative has to say**
   The three messages that work in a crowded feed, and the ones that quietly waste budget at peak CPMs.
2. **Where spend goes, and when to scale**
   Which offers deserve budget, and how to avoid bidding against everyone else on the same three days.
3. **Lowering blended CAC**
   Ads times offer times repeat. How the other two arms make the first one cheaper.

## Category 5: The Pre-Q4 Fix List

*What to tighten before pouring more money into traffic.*

1. **Conversion checks**
   The handful of on-site issues that cost the most during peak traffic.
2. **Retention checks**
   Making sure the second order actually happens.
